Privacy Policy

WRAITH Wallet · Last updated: July 2, 2026

WRAITH Wallet is a non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet. We do not collect, store, or have access to your funds, private keys, or recovery phrase at any time.

1. Who We Are

WRAITH Wallet ("WRAITH", "we", "us", "our") is a non-custodial, open-source-derived mobile cryptocurrency wallet application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and what it does not collect.

2. Non-Custodial Design

WRAITH does not operate servers that hold, transmit, or have access to your private keys, recovery phrase, or funds. Your wallet is generated and stored entirely on your device. We cannot see your balance, freeze your funds, recover a lost wallet, or access your assets under any circumstance. Losing your recovery phrase means permanent loss of access to your funds — WRAITH has no ability to restore it.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, email address, or any identity information (no account or sign-up is required to use WRAITH)
  • Your private keys or recovery phrase
  • Your wallet address linked to your real-world identity
  • Government ID or KYC documentation of any kind

4. Third-Party Services

To provide swap functionality, WRAITH connects to third-party liquidity and routing providers, currently including LI.FI, to fetch swap quotes and execute cross-chain and same-chain token swaps. When you use the swap feature, your wallet address and swap parameters (tokens, amounts) are sent to these providers to generate a quote and route the transaction. WRAITH does not control these third parties' data practices, and we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies. We are not responsible for how third-party providers handle data once it leaves the app.

WRAITH may also connect to blockchain nodes, block explorers, and WalletConnect relay servers to broadcast transactions and sync balances. These interactions are a normal function of any non-custodial wallet and do not involve WRAITH storing your data on our own servers, because we do not operate any.

5. Swap Fees

WRAITH charges a small, transparent fee (currently 1%) on swaps executed through the app. This fee is calculated automatically as part of the swap transaction and routed on-chain to a fee address controlled by the WRAITH developer. This is a transaction-level mechanism, not a data-collection mechanism — no personal information is exchanged as part of this process.

6. Analytics

WRAITH may use limited, anonymized, aggregate analytics to understand general app usage (such as crash reports or feature usage counts) in order to improve stability and performance. This data cannot be used to identify you individually and is never linked to your wallet address or holdings.

7. Children's Privacy

WRAITH is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app's functionality or legal requirements. Continued use of WRAITH after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
